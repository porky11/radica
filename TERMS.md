# Radica: Terms of Use, Disclaimer and Privacy

> **Draft, pending legal review.** This text is a good-faith summary of how Radica works and what to expect from it. It is not legal advice.

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

Radica is open-source software for a decentralized social network on [Freenet](https://freenet.org). By using it (the app, the donate page, or the code) you accept what follows.

- To the fullest extent the law allows, the authors and contributors are **not liable** for any damage, loss of data, loss of keys, loss of funds or other harm arising from using or being unable to use Radica.
- Features can change, break or be removed at any time. Supporter badges, donations and the Greenhouse currently run on a **test network** (Base Sepolia) and may be reset when Radica moves to Base mainnet.

## 2. No central service, no central moderation

- Radica has **no company, no servers of its own and no accounts**. Posts, messages and other content are signed by their authors and stored on the Freenet network, which nobody owns or controls.
- The authors of Radica **do not host, review or control** what users publish, and cannot delete content from the network.
- What you see is decided **on your own device**: by who you follow, the blocklists you trust, and checks that run on your computer. These tools reduce unwanted content; they do not guarantee it never appears.

## 3. Your responsibility

- **You are solely responsible for what you publish** and for complying with the laws that apply to you. Do not publish content that is illegal, that infringes others' rights, or that harms others.
- **Your identity is a key on your device.** Nobody can recover it for you: back it up. Anyone who gets your key can act as you.
- On a network with no owner, anyone who has seen your content may have kept a copy. **Deleting is not a guarantee** that content disappears. Publish as if it could last.

## 4. Donations and supporter badges

- Donations are **voluntary gifts** to support the development of Radica. They are **not a purchase, not an investment, and not refundable**. They give no ownership, profit, governance or other rights.
- The supporter badge, the trust it carries in the app and a voice in the Greenhouse are a **thank-you**, not a service or product in exchange for money. They can change or end.
- Donations go through a smart contract on a public blockchain. **Transactions are public**; the zero-knowledge proof hides which wallet paid for a given Radica identity, not that a donation took place.
- You are responsible for any tax or legal obligation that a donation may have where you live, and for using only funds you are allowed to use.

## 5. Greenhouse

Greenhouse votes show what supporters think. They are **a guide, not a promise**: the maintainers decide what is built, when, and whether at all.

## 6. Privacy

- Radica **does not collect personal data on any server** and has no analytics or trackers.
- Your key, settings and drafts stay on your device and on your own Freenet node.
- **Public by design:** posts, comments, community messages, your profile, your follows, Greenhouse seeds and votes (with your supporter tier), and, if you choose to appear there, your entry on the supporters wall.
- **Private messages and private rooms** are end-to-end encrypted: only their participants can read them. Their existence and timing may still be visible on the network.
- Translation, image and video checks run **on your device**; nothing you read or write is sent elsewhere for them.

## 7. Reporting illegal content

You can report a post in the app (it is hidden for you and for whoever trusts your blocklist). To report illegal content to the maintainers, open an issue at <https://github.com/scobru/radica/issues> or write to the maintainer persona in Radica. The maintainers will act on what they control (for example, moderation of the communities they run).

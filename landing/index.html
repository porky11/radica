Why Radica

Your voice, without a landlord.

Most social networks are one company's computers: they can read, sell, rank or delete what you say. Radica runs on Freenet, where every user's computer holds a piece of the network.

🌐

No servers

Your posts are signed by you and stored across Freenet. There is no central server to shut down or to sell your data.

🔑

You are a key

No email, no phone number, no password. Your identity is a cryptographic key on your device, or your Viso persona.

🛡️

Moderation on your device

Images and videos are checked on your computer before they are posted, and you choose whose blocklists to follow.

🌱

Rooted in community

Communities, private rooms, chat, polls and music, all open source. Anonymous supporter badges keep it independent.

Get started

Three steps, about five minutes.

A desktop or laptop is the easy way. On Android there is an unofficial app (tab below); there is nothing for iPhone yet.

Install Freenet

Freenet is the network Radica runs on. It installs like any app and keeps running quietly in the background, so your computer is part of the network.

Download Freenet for Windows

Run the installer and follow the wizard. Freenet starts as a background service with an icon in the system tray.

Download Freenet for macOS

Open the DMG and drag Freenet into Applications, then launch it. A rabbit icon appears in the menu bar, and Freenet starts by itself at login.

Open a terminal and paste:

curl -fsSL https://freenet.org/install.sh | sh

It installs Freenet and starts it as a background service that updates itself.

Get Freenet Android Node

A community app that runs a real Freenet node on your phone. Download the .apk from the latest release, allow your browser to install it, then open the app and press Start network node. Then try the Open Radica link below in your phone's browser.

Unofficial and early: not made or endorsed by the Freenet Project, and it can drain the battery. Each release lists checksums, and you can build it yourself.

Freenet is in alpha. It updates itself as the network evolves, and it sends diagnostic data to its developers to help fix the network. Don't use it for anything sensitive yet. Details on freenet.org.

Open Radica

With Freenet running, this link opens Radica from your own computer. Bookmark it: it is your address for Radica from now on.

Open Radica

Page not loading? Freenet is not running yet: start it from the tray (Windows), the menu bar (macOS) or with freenet service restart (Linux), wait a minute and try again.

Choose your identity

Radica opens on its Identity page. Pick one of the two ways in:

Sign in with Viso Recommended

Viso is your Freenet identity: one persona for Radica and other Freenet apps. Your key stays in Viso, and Radica only gets permission to post as you.

In Radica, press Sign in with Viso, make or pick a persona, and allow Radica.

Open Viso

A key just for Radica

Press Create New Keypair. Radica makes a key on your computer.

Back up the key it shows you. Pasting it on another device brings the same identity back; losing it means losing the identity.

Then follow a few people, join a community, and plant your first post. 🌱

Questions

Good to know

Is Radica free?

Yes. No ads and no subscription. Radica is open source, and anyone can read its code on GitHub.

Why do I have to install something?

On Freenet there is no company server to visit: your own computer is your way into the network. Radica then opens in your normal browser, from your computer.

Can I use it on my phone?

On Android, yes, with Freenet Android Node: an unofficial, early app that runs a Freenet node on the phone (see the Android tab in step 1). There is nothing for iPhone yet. The official Freenet installs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Who can see my posts?

Posts and communities are public on the network, like a public social network. Direct messages and private rooms are end-to-end encrypted: only the people in the chat can read them.

What if I lose my key?

Nobody can reset it for you: there is no company behind it. Keep the backup Radica shows you, or sign in with Viso, which keeps your persona for you.

Something doesn't work

Ask in the Freenet Matrix chat, or open an issue on Radica's GitHub.

Keep Radica independent

Donate USDC on Base through a contract nobody can change. A zero-knowledge proof gives you a supporter badge without revealing which wallet paid.
